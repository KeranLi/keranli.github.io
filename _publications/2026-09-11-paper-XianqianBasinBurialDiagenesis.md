This study combines carbonate clumped-isotope thermometry with apatite U-Th/He and fission-track thermochronology to constrain the thermal history of the Eocene Xianqian Basin in central Tibet. The burial temperatures are insufficient to explain the observed clumped-isotope temperatures through solid-state reordering alone; petrographic, mineralogical, and geochemical evidence instead indicates shallow-burial dolomitization. The resulting oxygen-isotope compositions record diagenetic fluids and cannot be treated as primary surface-water signals for paleoelevation reconstruction.
