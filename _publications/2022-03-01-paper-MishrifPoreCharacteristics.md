Using core descriptions, thin sections, gas measurements, mercury injection, and nuclear magnetic resonance data, this study compares the reservoir spaces and pore structures of the Mishrif-1 and Mishrif-3 intervals. It identifies strong facies controls on high-porosity and high-permeability reservoirs in reef and bioclastic shoal microfacies. Keran Li served as the corresponding author.
