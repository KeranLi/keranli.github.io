This study investigates how diagenetic fluids overprint stable-isotope signals in Eocene lacustrine micrite from the Gerze Basin on the Tibetan Plateau. By combining petrography, sequential acetic-acid leaching, carbonate and calcium isotopes, strontium isotopes, elemental ratios, and numerical modeling, the study separates a primary calcite end-member from two diagenetic dolomite end-members. The results show that unrecognized dolomitization can substantially bias paleo-elevation reconstructions.
