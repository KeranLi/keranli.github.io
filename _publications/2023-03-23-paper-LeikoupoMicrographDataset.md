This paper presents a 2.9 GB dataset containing 1,818 polarized-light micrographs and a thin-section identification table from six wells in the central Sichuan Basin. The dataset documents major Leikoupo Formation carbonate lithologies and supports sedimentary microfacies, reservoir, and machine-learning studies.
