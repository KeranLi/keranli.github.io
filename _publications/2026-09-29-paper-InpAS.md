This study introduces InpAS, a sedimentary knowledge-inspired deep-learning model for reconstructing arbitrary blank strips in formation micro-imager logs. The workflow combines a fine-tuned Segment Anything Model for gap detection with a diffusion network and Horizontal-Cross-Vertical-Relating attention guided by Walther's Law. Ten-fold evaluation shows improved image fidelity, structural similarity, and geological continuity compared with baseline inpainting models.
