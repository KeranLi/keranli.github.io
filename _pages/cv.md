Education
======

* **Ph.D. in Geology**, Nanjing University, 2023-present. Advisor: Prof. Xiumian Hu.
* **M.S. in Geology and Geological Engineering**, Chengdu University of Technology, 2020-2023. Advisor: Prof. Jinmin Song.
* **B.S. in Petroleum Geology**, Chengdu University of Technology, 2016-2020.

Research interests
======

Carbonate dolomitization; isotope geochemistry; sedimentary big data; carbonate stratigraphy; paleoaltimetry; deep-learning applications in geoscience.

Research profile
======

I work at the intersection of sedimentology, isotope geochemistry, and artificial intelligence. My research spans field investigation, laboratory measurements, multi-source database construction, machine-learning model development, and quantitative reconstruction of geological and environmental change.

Selected research projects
======

* **Mesozoic stratigraphic framework of the Qiangtang Basin**, National Key R&D Program, 2025-present. Field geology, zircon geochronology, carbonate U-Pb dating, clumped-isotope analysis, and Bayesian age modeling.
* **Mesozoic lithofacies-paleogeography and source-rock evaluation**, China National Offshore Oil Corporation-Nanjing University project, 2025-present. Project liaison and geochemical analysis across multiple study areas.
* **AI model development for petroleum geology**, China National Petroleum Corporation-Nanjing University doctoral project, 2025-2026. Project lead for detrital-particle image annotation, benchmarking, and the ChaNO model.
* **High-precision seismic signal classification**, China Earthquake Administration student research program, 2024-2025. Project lead for large-scale machine-learning and deep-learning evaluation.
* **Sedimentary material evolution**, Deep-time Digital Earth International Big Science Program, 2023-2024. Sedimentary-group coordinator and developer of an LLM-assisted detrital-zircon database.

Selected publications
======

I have published or co-authored **13 papers as first or corresponding author**, including work in *Earth System Science Data*, *Scientific Data*, *Computers & Geosciences*, *Petroleum Science*, and *IEEE Geoscience and Remote Sensing Letters*. Representative topics include:

* Carbonate and lake-system diagenesis, Ca-S-triple oxygen isotope proxies, and paleoaltimetry.
* Global detrital-zircon data infrastructure and large-scale sedimentary databases.
* Weakly supervised carbonate-image segmentation and deep-learning benchmarks.
* Graph-convolution and attention-based models for well-log lithology interpretation.
* Microbialite fabrics, carbonate reservoir characterization, and isotope geochemistry.

Laboratory and computational skills
======

* Carbonate, igneous-rock, and sediment sample preparation; Rb/Sr separation; TIMS operation.
* Ca, C, O, S, Sr, and other isotope-geochemistry workflows; carbonate U-Pb dating.
* Thin-section petrography, SEM, micro-CT, cathodoluminescence, mercury intrusion, and NMR analysis.
* Python-based data analysis; machine learning and deep learning; geoscience database construction; Bayesian age modeling.

Fieldwork and teaching
